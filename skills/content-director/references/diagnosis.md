# Diagnosis — question bank and what to save

Rounds of 3–4 questions with the multiple-choice tool. Open each round with what
you already know from Phase 0 so the person confirms instead of repeating.
Adapt options to what you found on disk. Never more than four questions at once.

## Round 1 — Who and what

- Is this a **personal brand** or a **company account**? (If company: who
  appears on camera — founder, employee, spokesperson, customers, nobody?)
- What does it offer, in one line, and where (country/cities)?
- Account links (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn). Read them in the
  browser: followers, posts, bio, what their own reels already do.
- Is there a brand manual, logo SVG, color/typography guide? Ask for the file.

## Round 2 — Audience and goal

- Who exactly: age band + situation ("18–30, first job, feels behind"), not
  "our audience".
- The one action wanted after watching: follow, DM, apply to a job, buy, book,
  visit the site. One.
- Which platforms, and whether each has a different audience (company example:
  workers on Instagram, clients on LinkedIn).
- Topics: offer 6–8 candidate pillars as a multi-select, derived from the
  business. Personal brands also get "which of these have you lived" (lived
  experience can be written in first person; the rest is observed or sourced).

## Round 3 — Voice and limits

- Register: tú / usted / vos; humor vs serious vs "depends on the topic".
- Language: local slang yes/no, mild swearing yes/no.
- Off-limits: politics, medical/psychological diagnosis, medication, competitor
  names, prices, legal promises… (multi-select, plus "other").
- Verified data that can be said on camera (years, clients, numbers,
  certifications) — each with a source. Anything else is `PENDIENTE`.
- How the brand appears in personal-brand content: only bio / soft mention /
  1 of every N videos.

## Round 4 — Resources

- Equipment (multi-select): wireless mic, tripod, LED/RGB light, practical
  lamps, second camera.
- Where they can film regularly (home, office, café, street).
- Time per video (under 1 h / 1–2 h / half day) and cadence (videos per week).
- Who edits: this kit (Remotion + ffmpeg), CapCut, an editor, nobody.
- On-camera comfort: always face / sometimes / faceless (voice-over, hands,
  screen).

## Round 5 — After the references (Phase 3)

- Which references represent them most (multi-select by name).
- Aspect ratio: vertical 9:16 (reach) vs cinematic horizontal inside vertical.
- Series: 1 or 2 named series; names (offer 4 in their tone, then more if
  asked); closing ritual.
- Image sources for support visuals: public-domain art, Pexels/Unsplash,
  Adobe Stock, their own photos/videos, film stills (flag copyright risk).

## Templates

### contenido/marca.md

```markdown
# Marca: <name>

- Tipo: personal / empresa. En cámara: <who>.
- Qué ofrece: <one line>. Dónde: <country, cities>.
- Cuentas: <links + followers at date>.
- Audiencia: <age band + situation>, por plataforma si cambia.
- Acción que buscamos: <one>.
- Temas: <pillars>. Con vivencia propia: <which>.
- Datos verificados (con fuente): <list>. PENDIENTE: <list>.
- Cómo aparece la marca/producto: <rule>.
- Recursos: equipo, locaciones, tiempo por video, cadencia, quién edita.
```

### contenido/reglas.md

```markdown
# Reglas (cada rechazo se vuelve regla, con fecha y palabras de la persona)

- Registro: <tú/usted/vos>, jerga <sí/no>, groserías <sí/no>.
- Nunca: <words, topics>.
- Cierre / CTA: <rule>.
- Silencios: nunca (diálogo continuo).
- <fecha> — "<cita de la persona>" → <regla>.
```
