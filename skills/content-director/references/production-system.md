# Production system — findings from 68 analysed references

Short-form references in Spanish and English (psychology, relationships,
self-development, creator education, marketing, culture), analysed shot by shot
in September 2026. Use this as the baseline to compare a new brand's references
against; update it when a new set says otherwise.

## The numbers

| Set | n | One or two camera takes | Median duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| High-production references chosen by a creator | 30 | 18 | 27 s |
| References saved by the same creator | 15 | 10 | 43 s |
| Account outliers (×10–×600 their own median) | 23 | 14 | 45 s |

**Production value is not cuts.** It comes from three layers:

### 1. Art direction in camera

- **Light:** one soft key facing the person (window or bounced LED) +
  **practical warm lamps visible in frame** + sometimes a **coloured
  background light** (teal/green, magenta). Darker, moodier images than a
  typical selfie (mean brightness ~0.45).
- **Background with a story:** café bookshelf, a wall of photos, a coloured
  studio sweep, a living room with plants and lamps.
- **Wardrobe as signature** (optional): the same shirt-and-tie, a statement
  jacket.
- **Wireless mic held in the hand**, visible: reads as "I'm talking to you".
- **Framing wider than a selfie** (seated, mid or full body) so graphics fit
  around the person. Camera slightly below eye level for essays.
- Some shoot at **24 fps** (film cadence) and in **16:9 or 4:3** for a cinematic
  look; vertical 9:16 still wins for reach.

### 2. Graphic layer

- **Cut-outs that appear when something is named:** public-domain paintings,
  film stills, editorial photos, stickers.
- **Evidence:** screenshots of real headlines and sources over the head.
- **Chapter cards:** a question on a plain colour with a roman numeral and thin
  spaced capitals; or big serif on grid paper. They can be full-screen (only if
  voice continues) or **overlays at the top while the person talks**.
- **Cards to save:** lists of ready-to-use phrases, steps, resources.
- **Key-word captions:** one big word instead of full subtitles, or tiny single
  words.
- **Numbering devices:** "1, 2, 3" as billiard balls, bold numbered titles,
  "Y tres,".
- **Game mechanics:** two labels at the top ("BÓRRALO / PUBLÍCALO") and examples
  flying into columns (tier list).

### 3. Script structure

- **Named, numbered series** with a recurring close ("Arte breve", "lunes de
  tecnicismos", "part 3", "001_CAP PILOTO", "Espero te sirva y nos vemos").
- Counted lists, chapter-questions, distinction theses, a reframe at the end.
- CTAs: keyword comment ("comenta LISTA"), follow for part N, tag/share.

## Typography systems that repeat

- **Serif italic + grotesk mix:** the word that matters in serif italic.
- **One huge uppercase grotesk word** (or song lyrics across the width).
- **Thin spaced capitals / typewriter** for chapter cards.
- **Condensed serif display** in red for scrapbook titles.
- **Rounded bold display** split either side of the face for a series title.
- **One accent colour per account:** cyan, yellow, red or cream. Never several.
- Subtitles: small, white or cream, one line, often on a dark pill when the
  wardrobe is light.

## Formats to replicate (ranked by how often they fit a personal/brand account)

| Format | Shape | Demands |
|---|---|---|
| Essay in 3 chapter-questions | A-roll + everyday B-roll, question overlays, reframe close | A place with character, 15 min of B-roll |
| Concept explained with art | Named concept, public-domain paintings pop in, numbered points | Image research; sourced facts |
| Utility with scrapbook | "You hate X because…", numbered rules, cards with phrases to copy | Graphic design time |
| List with evidence on a trend | "5 things worse than <trend>", headline screenshots | Real data with sources, a live trend |
| Distinction thesis + key word | One strong take, one big word per idea | Background/wardrobe with identity |
| Contrary instruction, short | "You need to X more", why, how, ritual close | A genuine opinion |
| Documentary pilot of yourself | "001", mantra, countdown, retro title card | A real story in progress |
| Tier list game | Two labels, examples fly in, keyword CTA | Examples to show |
| Text on loop | One line over a 6–11 s everyday clip | 5 minutes |
| Carousel of videos | Each slide a short clip, bold single-word captions | Several short takes |

## Copyright

Public-domain art and your own footage are safe. Stock (Pexels, Unsplash, Adobe
Stock) is safe within its licence. Film/series stills: short, still, transformed
(cropped, framed, graded), never full scenes with their audio — and tell the
person the risk.
