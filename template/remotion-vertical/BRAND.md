# Brand brief

Fill this in **once per company**, not once per video. Then paste it at the top
of any AI chat or agent session and the script comes back sounding like you
instead of like a press release.

Write it in whatever language you make videos in. Delete what doesn't apply.
Half-filled is still worth more than empty — but a blank line is better than a
guess, because a guess here becomes a claim in a published video.

> Llénalo en español si tus videos son en español. Lo importante es que el
> contexto viva aquí y no en una ventana de chat que se cierra.

---

## Who we are

One sentence. What the company does, for whom. No adjectives.

>

## Who this video is for

Specifically. "Our audience" is not an answer you can write from.

> Role / situation:
>
> What they already believe:
>
> What they'd have to hear to change their mind:

## What we want them to do

One action. Not "awareness."

>

## How we sound

Paste two or three **real** sentences from the company — a post that worked,
a line from the website, something a founder actually said. Worth more than
any list of adjectives.

>
>
>

## Words we never use

Ours, plus the ones that make any corporate video sound like every other one:
leading, innovative, end-to-end, synergy, empowering, seamless, best-in-class.

>

## Numbers and claims we are allowed to state

Only verified ones, with where they came from. Anything not on this list gets
asked about, never guessed.

## Things we must never claim

Regulated language, competitor comparisons, guarantees, client names without
written permission.

>

## Visual identity

| | |
|---|---|
| Primary color | `#` |
| Secondary color | `#` |
| Text on dark | `#` |
| Font | |
| Logo file | path, SVG or large clean PNG |

Take colors from the brand kit or a clean logo export — **never** sampled from
a screenshot or a video frame. Compression shifts them and the drift shows.

These go in `src/theme.ts`, nowhere else.

## Where these videos go

Platform, account, who publishes, who approves.

>

## Anything already decided

Strategy docs, a messaging framework, an earlier conversation where the
positioning got worked out, the campaign this belongs to. Paste it or link it.

This section is the point of the file: context that lives in a chat window gets
re-explained every time. Context that lives here doesn't.
